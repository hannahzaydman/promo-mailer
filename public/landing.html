How it works

Three steps to sent

01 — Recipients

Upload your DJ list

Drop in a spreadsheet (.xlsx or .csv) with DJ names and emails, or add contacts manually. DJ Promo auto-detects your columns.

02 — Releases

Attach your codes

Add one or more releases, each with its own Bandcamp download codes CSV. Write your subject and body using {name} and {code} placeholders.

03 — Send

Preview & send

Review every email before it goes out. Send a test to yourself first, then send all — straight from your own Gmail. Unused codes can be exported after.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Spreadsheet import

Upload .xlsx or .csv files. DJ Promo auto-guesses your name and email columns.

Multi-release campaigns

Send codes for multiple releases in a single campaign. Each release gets its own template and code pool.

Template library

Save and reload email templates across campaigns. Name templates and manage them per release.

Sends from your Gmail

Emails come from your own address — not a third-party bulk sender. Better deliverability, more personal.

Live send progress

Watch emails go out in real time with a per-recipient status log. Retry failed sends in one click.

Export unused codes

After sending, export any leftover download codes so nothing goes to waste.

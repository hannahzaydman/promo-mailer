DJ PROMO

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026

DJ Promo ("we", "us", or "our") operates djpromo.net, a tool that helps record labels and music promoters send promotional email campaigns to DJs using their own Gmail account. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive the following from Google's OAuth service:

  • Your name and email address (used to identify your account)
  • An OAuth access token and refresh token for your Gmail account (used solely to send emails on your behalf)

When you use the app, we also temporarily process:

  • Recipient lists (spreadsheet data) you upload — stored only for the duration of your session
  • Email subject lines and message bodies you compose
  • Bandcamp download codes you provide

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To send promotional emails via your connected Gmail account on your instruction
  • We do not read, index, or store the contents of your Gmail inbox
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties

3. Data Storage

Session data (including Gmail tokens) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the United States. Uploaded spreadsheet data is held in server memory only for the duration of an active session and is not written to disk or a database.

4. Google API Scopes

DJ Promo requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • openid, email, profile — to identify your account
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send — to send emails through your Gmail account

We request only the minimum permissions necessary. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention

Your session and Gmail tokens are retained until you sign out or your session expires. You may revoke access at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions and removing DJ Promo.

6. Security

We use HTTPS for all communications. OAuth tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to the browser. Sessions use signed, httpOnly cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

DJ Promo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at admin@midnightecstasy.com.
